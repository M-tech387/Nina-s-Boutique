About Nina's Boutique

Nina’s Boutique started in 2010, when Nyima Daffeh turned a lifelong love of tailoring into a Fashion Shop at Turntable. Fifteen years on, that same attention to detail is still behind every kaftan, grandmuba, and custom piece we make. We work in clothing styles for men and women, from ready-to-wear favourites to outfits cut to your exact measurements. Modern fits, hand-finished the traditional way — clothing that feels like it belongs to you. Every order gets the same care as our very first, whether it's an everyday piece or something made for a special occasion.

Nina’s Boutique — Where African culture meets timeless style.
