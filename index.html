About Nina’s Boutique

Founded in 2010 by Nyima Daffeh, Nina’s Boutique was built from a passion for African fashion, creativity, and the beauty of traditional clothing.

We specialize in African-inspired clothing, traditional outfits, kaftans, grandmuba styles, and custom-made designs for both men and women.

Our goal is more than simply selling clothes. We believe African fashion is a way to express identity, celebrate culture, and showcase personal style.

With years of experience, Nina’s Boutique combines traditional African influences with modern fashion to celebrate our heritage while keeping up with today's styles.

From ready-to-wear pieces to custom-made outfits tailored to your measurements, every customer is treated with care.

Nina’s Boutique — Where African culture meets timeless style.
